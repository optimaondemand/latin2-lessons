Write your own: turn the English “Iō, having been changed, was wandering” into one Latin sentence using a PPP that agrees with Iō. Then reveal one correct version.

Iō, mūtāta, errābat. — mūtāta is a PPP (nom. sg. f.) agreeing with Iō: “having been changed,” a completed passive action before the main verb errābat. If you wrote mūtāns (PAP) or mūtātūra (FAP), check voice and time against Card 2.
