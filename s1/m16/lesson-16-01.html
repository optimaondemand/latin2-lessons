📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Module 16 is the final content module of Semester 1 — and there is no new grammar to learn.
Instead, this module is your Semester 1 Grammar Review: a structured look back at everything you have built since Module 01, organized into reference cards you can use straight through finals week. Today we set the stage — the story of Iō, what this week will cover, and where Semester 2 is heading. Over the next four days you will revisit the six passive tenses, the three participles, the agent construction, and the translation method, then read the longest passage of the semester.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS MODULE YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
State all six passive indicative tenses and their 3rd-singular forms for amō.
2
Distinguish the three participles (PPP, PAP, FAP) by formation, voice, and time.
3
Apply the agent-construction rule: ā/ab + ablative for a personal agent; plain ablative for an instrument.
4
Use the four-step translation method for participial phrases.
5
Read and translate a ten-sentence Latin passage in which all Semester 1 grammar forms appear.
⏰ PART 1 · THE STORY REACHES EGYPT 10 min

Iō's journey ends in a transformation

Iō's story reaches its extraordinary conclusion here. Driven by Juno's gadfly across the seas to Egypt, she falls as a supplicant before Jupiter, who persuades Juno to relent. Iō is restored to human form, becomes queen of Egypt, and is eventually venerated as the goddess Isis. Her journey — from river nymph to cow to wandering exile to divine queen — is the ultimate Ovidian mūtātiō.

🏛️ THE MŪTĀTIŌ AT THE END OF SEMESTER 1

Iō's story ends with a transformation back to human form — and then to a goddess. As you review the grammar of Semester 1, notice how each form you learned — every passive tense, every participial phrase — has been in service of describing transformation. The passage you will read this week is the longest of the semester: ten sentences, rich with passive tenses across all six forms, participial phrases (PPP, PAP, and FAP), and agent constructions. It is the ideal preparation for the final exam.
