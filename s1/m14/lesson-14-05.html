✅ PART 2 · MIXED PRACTICE 20 min

Five mixed reps, drawing on the whole module — forming, identifying, and translating in one place. Then build an answer of your own.

✅ TRY IT · form

✅ TRY IT · identify

✅ TRY IT · translate
Translate: Iuppiter fulmen iactūrus erat.
✅ TRY IT · agreement
To say “the queen, about to fall,” which FAP form modifies rēgīna (nom. sg. f.)?
✅ TRY IT · vocabulary
What does fulmen, fulminis mean?

Commit first, then check: give all three participles of mittō (PPP missus) with their meanings. Write them, then reveal.

PPP missus — “having been sent” (passive, past). PAP mittēns, mittentis — “sending” (active, simultaneous). FAP missūrus, -a, -um — “about to send” (active, future). Same verb, full timeline of action.
