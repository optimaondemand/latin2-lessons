📚 PART 2 · THE PARADIGM 18 min

Second conjugation present passive: moneō

Here is the model verb moneō, monēre (to warn) laid out active-beside-passive, so you can see the endings swap while the stem holds.

PersonActivePresent passiveMeaning (passive)
1st sgmoneōmoneorI am warned
2nd sgmonēsmonērisyou are warned
3rd sgmonetmonēturhe/she/it is warned
1st plmonēmusmonēmurwe are warned
2nd plmonētismonēminīyou (pl.) are warned
3rd plmonentmonenturthey are warned

1st conjugation — amā-

amātur — he is loved
tenētur? — no: that's 2nd.

Look for the long ā.

2nd conjugation — monē-

monētur — he is warned
tenētur — he is held
movētur — he is moved

Look for the long ē.
💡 THE ONE SHORTENING

The long ē survives before almost every ending — monētur, monēmur, monēminī. But it shortens before -ntur in the 3rd plural: monentur, not *monēntur. It's the same short e you already see in the active monent (“they warn”).

Notice too that the 1st singular is moneor — the -or ending, just as in 1st conjugation amor. That is the one form where the stem vowel shortens at the very start as well.
