📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
This is finals week. There is nothing new to learn — only a whole semester to gather back up.
Module 17 is the capstone of Latin 2, Semester 1. It contains no new grammar, no new vocabulary, and no new instruction — this week you review everything from Modules 1–16 and complete the two final assessments. Today's page is your map: what the semester covered, what you'll be graded on, and how the next four review days are laid out. By the end of the week you will sit the S1 Final Exam and submit an original Transformation Story in Latin.

⏰ PART 1 · LOOK HOW FAR YOU'VE COME 6 min

A whole semester of Latin

Latin 1 taught you to make sentences act. This semester taught you to let things be acted upon — and to fold whole clauses into single words. Everything on the final grew out of three big moves:

What Latin 1 gave you

The active voice — a subject doing something.
Perseus mōnstrum necat.
Perseus kills the monster.

What Semester 1 added

The passive voice — a subject receiving the action.
Mōnstrum ā Perseō necātur.
The monster is killed by Perseus.

📚 PART 2 · WHAT THE FINAL COVERS 12 min

The three grammar pillars — and the vocabulary

The S1 Final Exam is cumulative over Modules 1–16. Everything it tests fits under three grammar pillars plus your vocabulary:

PillarWhat it isReviewed on
Passive voiceAll six tenses: present, imperfect, future, perfect, pluperfect, future perfectDay 2
The participlesThree of them: PPP (perfect passive), PAP (present active), FAP (future active)Day 3
Agent constructionā/ab + ablative for a person; plain ablative for a thing (means)Day 2
VocabularyCumulative words from every module, M01–M16All week · Vocabulary Review
💡 EXAM PREP TIPS

Focus your study on the pillars above. Pay special attention to all six passive tenses, the three participles (their formation, voice, time, and translation), and agent vs. means (ā/ab + ablative for personal agent; plain ablative for the instrument or means).

Reread your Grammar Quiz corrections from every module — exam items are similar in format — and expect cumulative vocabulary from all sixteen modules.

✅ PART 3 · THE TWO ASSESSMENTS 14 min

What you will be graded on

Finals week has two graded pieces. Both are due before the end of Week 36 — check Canvas for exact dates and submission links.

📝 ASSESSMENT 1 · S1 FINAL EXAM · 100 PTS · AUTO-GRADED

A comprehensive auto-graded exam covering all grammar from M01–M16: the complete passive voice system (all six tenses), the three participles (PPP, PAP, and FAP), agent construction, and cumulative vocabulary from the entire semester. Check Canvas for the exam availability window; there is no fixed time limit listed here.

✎ ASSESSMENT 2 · TRANSFORMATION STORY · 100 PTS · TEACHER-REVIEWED

A creative writing project. Write an original myth in Latin (minimum 8 sentences) using at least one transformation as the central event. Your story must include at least two examples of the passive voice and at least one participial phrase.

The full rubric is available in Canvas under S1 Syllabus. We plan the story in detail on Day 4, right after the myth review.
