✅ TRY IT · build the form
Make mūtāre say “he/she/it is changed” (present passive).

Write your own: make one full Latin sentence meaning “The nymph was changed by the goddess.” Use a perfect passive verb and a personal-agent phrase. Then reveal one correct version.

Nympha ā deā mūtāta est. — nympha (subject) + ā deā (personal agent, ā + ablative) + mūtāta est (perfect passive; the PPP agrees with the feminine subject). If you wrote deā without ā, remember: a person needs ā/ab.

📝 TRANSFORMATION STORY — A REMINDER OF THE REQUIREMENTS

Write a myth in which a character undergoes a transformation, as Daphne, Narcissus, Arachne, Pyramus and Thisbe, Midas, Actaeon, Phaethon, and Io all did this semester. Your story must:

  • Be written in Latin, minimum 8 sentences.
  • Include a clear transformation (mūtātiō) as the central event.
  • Use at least 2 passive voice verbs (any tense).
  • Use at least 1 participial phrase (PPP, PAP, or FAP).
  • Have a beginning, middle, and end — follow the Ovidian narrative arc.

Full rubric: see S1 Syllabus in Canvas.
