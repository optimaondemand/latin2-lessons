✅ PART 2 · MIXED PRACTICE 20 min

Five mixed reps drawing on the whole module — forming, identifying, and translating passive verbs. Then build a form of your own.

✅ TRY IT · name the tense
Mīdās laudābātur ā cīvibus suīs. The verb laudābātur is…
✅ TRY IT · name the tense
Harēna aurea in rīpā relicta erit. The verb relicta erit is…
✅ TRY IT · name the tense
Mīdās ā fluviō lavātus est. The verb lavātus est is…
✅ TRY IT · build the form
Make the PPP solūt- say “it (n.) will have been lifted.” (3rd sg n., agreeing with maledictum)
✅ TRY IT · translate
Translate: Oppida capta erant.

Write your own: give the future perfect passive of mūnīre (PPP mūnītus) meaning “the road will have been built” (via, f. sg.). Then reveal one correct version.

Via mūnīta erit. — PPP mūnīta (f. sg., agrees with via) + erit (future of esse). If you wrote mūnītus or used est/erat, check agreement and the esse form: feminine subject needs mūnīta, and future perfect needs erit.
