✅ PART 2 · MIXED PRACTICE 20 min

Five mixed reps, drawing on the whole week — conjugating, parsing, and translating in one place. Then build a form of your own. Wrong answers give a hint; use Reset to try again.

✅ TRY IT · conjugate
Make spectāre say “you (pl.) are watched.”
✅ TRY IT · parse
In Nymphae laudantur, what person, number, and voice is laudantur?
✅ TRY IT · translate
Translate: Daphne ab Apolline amātur.
✅ TRY IT · the odd form
Which form means “I am wounded”?
✅ TRY IT · read the ending
Amāmur. What does this form mean?

Write your own: turn the active sentence Apollo Daphnen spectat (“Apollo watches Daphne”) into a passive one meaning “Daphne is watched by Apollo.” Then reveal one correct version.

Daphne ab Apolline spectātur. — the receiver Daphne becomes the nominative subject; the doer moves into ab Apolline (“by Apollo”); and the verb takes the passive ending: spectātur (3rd sg, “is watched”).
