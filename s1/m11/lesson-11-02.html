★ One Stem, One Ending: -ns / -ntis
Lesson · about 40 minutes
📖 TODAY'S FOCUS
Yesterday you learned what the PAP means. Today you learn how to build one.
The recipe is short: take the present stem, add -ns for the nominative singular and -ntis for the genitive. That -ntis tells you it declines as a 3rd declension adjective. Unlike the PPP's three forms (-us, -a, -um), the PAP has just one nominative singular for all genders — amāns, not three versions of it.
🎯 BY THE END OF THIS LESSON YOU WILL BE ABLE TO…
1
I will be able to form the PAP (nom. and gen. singular) for verbs of all four conjugations.
2
I will be able to decline a PAP through the 3rd declension — nominative, accusative, genitive, ablative, singular and plural.
3
I will be able to recognize the 3rd conjugation's linking -ē- before -ns / -ntis.

📚 PART 2 · FORMATION ACROSS THE CONJUGATIONS 12 min

Present stem + -ns / -ntis

The PAP is built on the present stem. The nominative singular ends in -ns; the genitive singular ends in -ntis. The ending interacts slightly differently with each conjugation, but the signature is always the same: an -ns you can confirm with a genitive -ntis.

ConjugationExample verbPAP nom. sg.PAP gen. sg.Meaning
1stamō, amāreamānsamantisloving
2ndmoneō, monēremonēnsmonentiswarning
3rdmittō, mitteremittēnsmittentissending
3rd -iōcapiō, caperecapiēnscapientistaking
4thaudiō, audīreaudiēnsaudientishearing
💡 THE 3RD CONJUGATION QUIRK

3rd conjugation verbs slip an -ē- in before -ns / -ntis: mitt- → mittēns, mittentis. Compare tremō → tremēns, trementis (“trembling”) and currō → currēns, currentis (“running”). The 3rd -iō and 4th conjugations both use -iēns, -ientis.

📌 PART 3 · DECLINING THE PAP 8 min

It declines like a 3rd declension adjective

Here is amāns, amantis partly declined. All genders share the same forms; only the noun it modifies tells you the gender. The accusative singular amantem is the key form for spotting a PAP used as a direct object or object complement.

CaseSingular (all genders)Plural (all genders)
Nom.amānsamantēs
Acc.amantemamantēs
Gen.amantisamantium
Abl.amante (or -ntī)amantibus
