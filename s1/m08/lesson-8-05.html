✅ TRY IT · source
Which principal part must you know to build a verb's PPP?

Write your own: form the phrase meaning “the wounded lovers” (m. pl. nom.), using the PPP of vulnerō (4th part vulnerātum) with amāntēs. Then reveal one correct version.

amāntēs vulnerātī — the wounded lovers. The PPP vulnerātī is m. pl. nom., agreeing with the plural subject. If you wrote vulnerātus or vulnerāta, check agreement: the PPP must match its noun in gender, number, and case.
